Project Case Study

My Spotify Listening Analysis Dashboard

A two-page Power BI dashboard analyzing personal Spotify listening history — built end-to-end from raw export data to an interactive, published report.

Power BI Python / pandas DAX Power Query Data Storytelling

Overview

This project turns raw personal Spotify streaming history into an interactive Power BI dashboard. It covers the full analytics workflow: requesting and exporting the data from Spotify, cleaning and reshaping it with Python, modeling it in Power BI, and designing a two-page report with custom DAX measures, a day-by-hour listening heatmap, and bookmark-driven page navigation.

Objective

Answer two core questions from my own listening data:

  • How much am I listening, and how is that changing year over year? (volume & trend)
  • When and how do I listen — which days, hours, devices, and behaviors (skipping, shuffling) define my habits? (behavior & pattern)

These two questions map directly to the dashboard's two pages: Snapshot and Habits.

Data Source & Preparation

Data source: Spotify's official "Extended Streaming History" export (requested via Spotify Account Privacy settings), covering multiple years of listening activity at the individual-stream level — track, artist, album, timestamp, milliseconds played, platform, and skip/shuffle flags.

Cleaning & transformation (Python / pandas, in Jupyter Notebook):

  • Combined multiple yearly JSON exports into a single dataset
  • Parsed timestamps and derived date, hour, day-of-week, month, and year fields
  • Converted milliseconds played into minutes for readability
  • Filtered out plays under 30 seconds to exclude accidental/skipped taps
  • Renamed Spotify's raw technical field names into clean, dashboard-friendly labels
  • Exported the cleaned dataset to CSV for import into Power BI

Data Model

The model follows a simple star-schema pattern:

  • Spotify — the cleaned fact table of individual streams (one row per play)
  • DateTable — a dedicated calendar table (CALENDAR() in DAX) related to Spotify on date, enabling proper time-intelligence and correct chronological/weekday sorting
  • Measures — a dedicated table holding all DAX measures, organized into display folders (KPIs, Top Content, Trend Measures, Habits & Rates) for a clean, navigable Fields pane

Key DAX techniques used include TOPN() for dynamic "top artist/track" lookups, DATEADD() for year-over-year comparisons, and SWITCH(TRUE()) with CONTAINSSTRING() to clean and group messy raw platform/device strings into simple Android vs. Desktop categories.

Page 1 — Snapshot

The first page answers "how much and what": overall listening volume, year-over-year trend, and top content.

Page 1 — Snapshot: KPI cards with year-over-year variance, yearly trend line, and Top 10 artist/track rankings.
  • Five KPI cards (Hours Listened, Unique Tracks, Unique Artists, Days Active, Total Plays), each showing a year-over-year comparison arrow and percentage change
  • A yearly trend line tracking total minutes played across the full listening history
  • Top 10 Artists and Top 10 Songs, ranked by total minutes played
  • A year-range slicer to filter the entire page

Page 2 — Habits

The second page answers "when and how": listening patterns by time and by device, plus real listening behavior.

Page 2 — Habits: day × hour listening heatmap, skip/shuffle behavior by day, and platform split.
  • A day-by-hour heatmap (conditional formatting) showing exactly when listening activity peaks across the week
  • A paired horizontal bar chart totaling minutes by hour, reinforcing the heatmap's pattern
  • Skip Rate and Shuffle Rate KPI cards, plus Most Active Day and Most Active Hour
  • A Skip vs. Shuffle Rate by Day chart, surfacing behavioral patterns across the week
  • A Platform Split donut chart comparing Android vs. Desktop listening

Navigation & Design

Both pages share a consistent dark, Spotify-branded theme (near-black backgrounds, Spotify green accents) and an identical header: a page title, a custom toggle-pill navigation control, and a shared year slicer.

  • Navigation between pages uses Power BI bookmarks: two custom-built toggle buttons (styled as rounded pill shapes with a status dot) are linked to bookmarks via the Action format setting, so clicking either one jumps to and highlights the corresponding page — without any native page-tab navigation visible
  • Conditional formatting is used throughout (heatmap cell shading, KPI variance colors) rather than static colors, so the dashboard visually reacts to the underlying data

Key Insights

  • Listening volume grew steadily year over year, with a notable peak in the most recent full year
  • Listening activity clusters around two daily peaks — morning and early evening hours — consistent across most weekdays
  • The majority of listening happens on Desktop rather than Android, a split visible at a glance via the Platform Split donut
  • Skip and shuffle behavior both vary meaningfully by day of week, suggesting different listening contexts
